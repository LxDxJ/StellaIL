Minigames

Quick games you can play right in Discord, for dollars, gold and the occasional rare card.

🧠 Guess the character

/guess · gs

A character's picture appears and everyone in the channel races to name them. After 20 seconds the series is given as a hint, and you have 60 seconds in total. The first right answer wins $1,000. Each player can start a round every 10 minutes.

Free to play

🃏 Memory match

mm

Find all 6 pairs on a 3×4 grid. A perfect game takes 6 turns and pays $1,000; every extra turn takes $100 off, down to a minimum of $200.

Free to play

🎲 Gamble

/gamble <amount> · gb 500

Put in some dollars or gold and the result is quick: you double it, lose it, or hit the ×4 jackpot. Up to $100,000 or 50 gold per bet, with a 10 second wait between bets.

Bet dollars or gold

♠️ Blackjack

/blackjack <bet> · bj 500

A hand against the dealer with Hit, Stand and Double buttons. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. A blackjack pays 3:2.

Bet dollars or gold

🂡 Four card poker

/poker <bet> · pkr 500

You get 5 cards and keep the best 4; the dealer gets 6 and keeps 4. The better hand wins.

Bet dollars or gold

🎡 Roulette

/roulette · r

Spin for 5 gold (single or 10x). Prizes range from dollars and gold to gems, event chests and rare cards, up to a print #1.

Costs 5 gold a spin

Type the command in any channel where Stella is, or use the slash version. Games that use your money show the bet before you start, and the cooldown between bets keeps things fair. Please play for fun and only with what you're happy to lose. New here? Type /help in Discord and add a word to search, like help roulette.
