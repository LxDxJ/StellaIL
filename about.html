About Stella

Stella is a card collection game that lives on Discord. It started as a small project made for friends and grew into a community with players and servers all over Discord.

The idea is simple: collect cards of any character or object you like, from anime to anything else, and show off your collection.

Stella is built and run by one developer, so updates come steadily rather than all at once. Player feedback shapes what gets added next. Come say hi in the community server.
