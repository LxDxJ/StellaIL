Collect cards.
Any character. Any theme.

Stella is a card collection game on Discord. Drop cards, grab your favorites, trade with friends and build the collection nobody else has.

Add Stella to your server Join the community

How it works

1

Drop

Type /drop and three cards land in the channel. Everyone can see them.

2

Grab

Press a button to claim the one you want. If two people go for the same card, a fight decides it.

3

Collect

Every card has its own print number and rarity. Fill albums, trade, and chase the low prints.

What you can do

🃏 Every theme

Anime, games, movies, objects, whatever. If it can be a card, it can be in Stella.

📚 Albums & events

Complete a series to finish its album. Limited events bring cards that only exist for a while.

🤝 Trade & auctions

Swap with friends, gift cards, or bid in auctions. Every card keeps its full history.

🛡️ Clans & wars

Team up, hit weekly goals together, open the clan chest and fight clan wars.

🎨 Make it yours

Frames, dyes, fonts and foils for your cards, and a home you can decorate from Discord or the website.

⚖️ Fair for everyone

Supporting Stella never makes you stronger than other players. See Support Stella.

Popular commands

/drop

Drop 3 cards in the channel. Anyone can grab one.

/daily

Free dollars and gold every day, plus a dye every 7th day in a row.

/collection

Browse and filter your cards by series, rarity, print and more.

/view

Look at one card up close with its print, owner and history.

/guess

Name the character from the picture and win a prize.

/clan view

Your clan's goal, chest, members and war.
