Changelog

What is new in Stella and its website

15 November 2026 · Website

  • A new home page with live numbers (servers, players, cards, characters, series, clans), a strip of cards, how it works, the running event and the top clans.
  • New Commands page: every player command with its typed shortcuts, searchable. It reads the bot's own help list, so it stays up to date.
  • New Series pages: browse every series and see its characters, cards collected, collectors and completed albums.
  • New Clans page: top clans by all-time points, weekly points, war wins, members and cards, plus the wars running right now. Anyone can see it, no login needed.
  • New Characters browser: search every character and series and see each edition's art. Public.
  • New Events page: running and past events with their cards. Upcoming events stay hidden until they start.
  • New My achievements page with progress bars, rewards and a filter for what is still to do.
  • New My clan page: members, weekly goal, and (for leaders and officers) settings, roles and kicking. Money actions stay in Discord.
  • More leaderboards on the Stats page: unique characters, #1 prints, legendaries, achievements, drops, grabs, bond points and max bonds.
  • Albums can now be searched, filtered (in progress, complete, not started) and sorted.
  • Card decorator, card tags with sorting, and a home editor on the website, each with a preview, the price and a confirm step before anything is spent.
  • The menus are tidier: an Explore menu for public pages and a My Stella menu for your own pages.
  • The Minigames page was removed; the games themselves are unchanged and live in Discord.

14 November 2026 · Bot and website

  • Albums only count slots you can actually get: released editions, running events, and finished events whose chest is still open.
  • First version of the website: log in with Discord to see your albums, collection stats, achievements and card pages. We only get your Discord name and avatar.
  • /invite now includes a link to the website.
