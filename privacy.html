Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what data Stella ("the bot") and its website ("the website") store, why, and how you can have it deleted. It's part of our Terms of Service.

The bot is a free card-collecting game on Discord, run by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: rishuriken@gmail.com or our support server.

2. What we collect

The bot only stores what it needs to run the game:

DataWhy
Discord user IDTo link your game progress to you
Cards, silver, gold, extrasYour collection and balances
Cooldown times, daily missions, activity counts (drops, grabs, trades…)To run cooldowns, missions, profiles and leaderboards
Profile settings (bio, color, favorite card and series), wishlists, card tags, reminder settingsFeatures you set up yourself
Trades, gifts, market listings and sales, card ownership historyTo run the market and trades, show card history, and investigate scams
Character suggestions and the images you submitTo review and add new cards
Server and channel IDsTo know where drops are allowed and where to send reminders
Ban recordsTo enforce our rules
Supporter status (tier, since when) and the Discord username you give us when you donateTo give you your supporter role and perks
How many times you used Stella's AI chat todayTo enforce the daily limit

3. What we don't collect

  • Your message content. The bot reads messages in channels it can see only to check whether they start with one of its quick commands (like "sd"). Messages that aren't commands are ignored right away, and no message content is stored (the AI chat in section 4 is the one exception).
  • Passwords, emails or payment details. The bot never asks for them. Donations are handled by a third-party payment provider, so we never see your card or bank details.
  • Your server's member list or anything about people who don't use the bot.

4. How it's used and who can see it

We use this data only to run and moderate the game. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything outside the bot.

The game is shared across every server the bot is in, so other players can see:

  • your collection, profile, albums and wishlist
  • your market listings, and your name on trades, gifts and sales
  • which cards you own in a card's history and in lookups (such as lowest prints)
  • your rank on leaderboards

The bot may send you DMs, for example when a market listing sells or for reminders you turned on.

Bot admins can see trade, sale and moderation logs to investigate problems.

The website. You can log in on Stella's website with Discord to see your collection and stats. The site is for logged-in players only for now.

  • What we receive. Discord's login (OAuth2) gives us only your Discord user ID, username and avatar. We never receive your password, email address or server list, and we can't act on your Discord account. Discord's access token is discarded right after login.
  • Cookies. We set one signed session cookie on your device to keep you logged in. It lasts up to 7 days or until you log out. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • What the site shows. Once you're logged in, the site shows data the bot already stores: your own collection, balances and profile, plus server-wide stats, card lookups and leaderboards. Leaderboards show other players' Discord names and ranks, the same as in the bot.
  • Technical data. The public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the logged-in pages are served by the hosting service that runs the bot. These services process visitors' IP addresses and requests to deliver the site, under their own privacy policies. We may briefly use your IP address in memory to limit abuse and don't store it in our database.

Services we use.

  • AI chat. If you talk to Stella with the AI chat (stella <message>), the text of your message and the earlier messages in that conversation are sent to Anthropic, which writes the reply. We don't save these messages in our database.
  • top.gg. When you use /vote, we send your Discord user ID to top.gg to check whether you voted.
  • Donations. Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive only the details needed to give you your perks.
  • Hosting. The bot runs on a hosting service we rent.

5. Storage and keeping your data

Data is stored in the bot's database on a server or hosting service we use. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure. We keep your data while you play, and until you ask us to delete it.

6. Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, contact us at rishuriken@gmail.com or in our support server with your Discord username. We'll remove your account data, including your cards, balances and settings. Records others depend on, such as another player's card history or ban records, may keep your user ID. Logging out of the website clears its cookie.

7. Children

The bot follows Discord's minimum age (at least 13). We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under that age uses the bot, contact us and we'll delete their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: rishuriken@gmail.com or our support server. See also our Terms of Service.
